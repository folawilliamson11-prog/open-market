Project overview

A clearer and more trustworthy marketplace

OpenMarket is an independent portfolio concept demonstrating product strategy, customer-centred design, interaction design and accessible visual design.

The opportunity

Online shoppers can face overwhelming product choices, inconsistent seller information, unclear delivery expectations and complicated checkout journeys.

OpenMarket explores how a marketplace could reduce these difficulties while maintaining the range and flexibility expected from modern e-commerce platforms.

Design objective

Create a responsive marketplace that helps customers find relevant products, assess seller reliability and complete purchases confidently.

The challenge

Removing uncertainty from discovery and checkout

The experience must support quick decisions while remaining understandable for customers with different levels of digital confidence.

01

Complex discovery

Customers need clear search, familiar categories and useful filters that reduce large product selections.

02

Trust concerns

Seller ratings, delivery information, returns and payment protection must be visible before purchase.

03

Checkout friction

Long forms, unexpected costs and unclear progress can cause customers to abandon their baskets.

Design process

From product problem to accessible interface

The project follows a structured product-design process. Its findings are presented as design hypotheses because this is an independent concept.

01

Discover

Reviewed common marketplace experiences and identified possible friction involving discovery, trust, delivery and payment.

02

Define

Organised observations into customer needs, product opportunities and a focused problem statement.

03

Develop

Developed user flows, information architecture, wireframes and reusable interface patterns.

04

Evaluate

Reviewed the proposed experience against usability, responsiveness and accessibility requirements.

User perspective

Designing for confident decision-making

A provisional persona helped keep the concept focused on customer needs throughout the design process.

Amara

Busy mobile marketplace customer

Primary goal

Find a suitable product from a dependable seller and complete the purchase without hidden costs or confusing steps.

Important needs

  • Clear product and delivery information
  • Useful filters and comparison options
  • Visible seller ratings and reviews
  • Secure payment and simple checkout
  • Accessible mobile content

Interface designs

Mobile-first OpenMarket screens

The concepts demonstrate product discovery, categories, product details, the shopping basket and checkout.

Inclusive design

Accessibility considered from the beginning

The proposed design uses understandable content, visible focus states and responsive layouts.

  • Meaningful headings and page structure
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation
  • Visible keyboard-focus indicators
  • Clear colour contrast
  • Descriptive labels and alternative text
  • Responsive mobile and desktop layouts

Outcome and reflection

A scalable marketplace foundation

What the concept demonstrates

OpenMarket demonstrates how product strategy, research assumptions, information architecture, interaction design and accessible visual design can form a unified marketplace experience.

The next stage would involve moderated usability testing with genuine customers, followed by design revisions and accessibility testing with assistive technologies.

Portfolio disclosure OpenMarket is an independent conceptual project. It is not commissioned work for Amazon, Aviva, MTN Nigeria, Nigerian Bottling Company or another employer. The proposed outcomes are design objectives rather than verified commercial results.
